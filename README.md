Project Description

FeeWise is a responsive web-based School Payment Breakdown Calculator developed using HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.

The application is designed to help students calculate, organize, and monitor their school-related expenses.

Students often have different financial obligations, including school fees, acceptance fees, departmental dues, faculty dues, accommodation fees, library fees, medical fees, and other academic charges.

Calculating these expenses manually can be time-consuming and may lead to errors.

FeeWise provides a simple and interactive platform where students can enter their school expenses, calculate the total amount required, record payments, and monitor outstanding balances.

The application also includes an instalment payment planner that helps students estimate how they can distribute their outstanding school expenses over a selected number of months.

FeeWise provides a dashboard displaying total school expenses, amounts paid, outstanding balances, and payment completion percentages.

Students can generate a printable payment breakdown report showing their school expenses and recorded payments.

The application uses browser Local Storage to save school fee records, payment information, student profiles, and theme preferences.

FeeWise is a financial planning and calculation application. It does not process actual school payments, verify transactions, or generate official school receipts.

Application Features

1. Dashboard

FeeWise provides a dashboard that displays important financial information.

The dashboard includes:

* Total School Fees
* Amount Paid
* Outstanding Balance
* Payment Progress
* School Fee Breakdown
* Quick Action Buttons

The dashboard automatically updates when students add fees or record payments.

2. School Fee Calculator

The application allows students to enter different school expenses.

Available fee categories include:

* School Fees
* Acceptance Fee
* Departmental Dues
* Faculty Dues
* Accommodation
* Library Fee
* Medical Fee
* ICT Fee
* Examination Fee
* Other Fees

Students can enter the amount and an optional description for each fee.

The application automatically calculates the total amount.

3. Custom Fee Categories

FeeWise allows students to create custom school fee categories.

Students can:

* Select the Other Fees option.
* Enter a custom fee name.
* Enter the fee amount.
* Add an optional description.
* Save the fee category.

4. Automatic Fee Calculation

FeeWise automatically calculates the total school expenses.

For example:

Fee Category	Amount
School Fees	₦75,000
Acceptance Fee	₦20,000
Departmental Dues	₦5,000
Faculty Dues	₦3,000
Accommodation	₦35,000
Library Fee	₦2,000
Medical Fee	₦5,000
Total	₦145,000

The application calculates:

Total School Fees = Sum of All Recorded Fee Amounts

All monetary values are displayed in Nigerian Naira (₦).

5. Payment Tracker

FeeWise provides a payment tracking system.

Students can:

* Select a recorded school fee.
* Enter the amount paid.
* Select the payment date.
* Add an optional payment note.
* Save the payment record.
* View previous payment entries.
* Delete payment records.

The application prevents students from recording payments that exceed the outstanding balance of a fee.

6. Outstanding Balance Calculator

The application automatically calculates outstanding balances.

The formula is:

Outstanding Balance = Total School Fees − Total Amount Paid

For example:

Total School Fees = ₦145,000

Amount Paid = ₦85,000

Outstanding Balance = ₦60,000

The dashboard automatically updates when a payment is recorded.

7. Payment Progress Indicator

FeeWise displays the percentage of school expenses recorded as paid.

The formula is:

Payment Progress = (Amount Paid ÷ Total Fees) × 100

The application displays the calculated percentage using a visual progress bar.

8. Fee Payment Status

Each school fee is assigned a payment status.

The available statuses are:

* Paid: The full amount has been recorded as paid.
* Partial: Part of the fee has been recorded as paid.
* Unpaid: No payment has been recorded.

The payment status updates automatically.

9. Instalment Payment Planner

FeeWise includes an instalment planner that allows students to estimate how they can distribute outstanding school expenses across several months.

Available planning periods include:

* 2 Months
* 3 Months
* 4 Months
* 5 Months
* 6 Months
* 9 Months
* 12 Months

For example:

Outstanding Balance = ₦60,000

Number of Months = 3

Estimated Monthly Payment = ₦20,000

The application generates a payment schedule showing:

* Instalment Number
* Estimated Payment Date
* Payment Amount
* Remaining Balance

The instalment planner is intended for personal budgeting and does not guarantee that an institution accepts instalment payments.

10. Payment History

The application allows students to view previously recorded payments.

Payment records include:

* Fee Category
* Amount Paid
* Payment Date
* Payment Note

Students can also delete individual payment records.

11. Payment Summary Report

FeeWise provides a detailed payment summary.

The report displays:

* Student Name
* Institution
* Department
* Academic Level
* Total School Fees
* Amount Paid
* Outstanding Balance
* Detailed Fee Breakdown

12. Printable Payment Summary

Students can print their school payment breakdown using the browser’s printing feature.

The application includes a print-friendly layout.

The report can also be saved as a PDF using a browser’s Print → Save as PDF option.

The generated report is a personal expense summary, not an official school receipt or proof of payment.

13. Expense Distribution Chart

FeeWise provides a horizontal bar chart displaying the percentage contribution of each school fee to the total expenses.

The chart updates automatically when fee categories are added or removed.

14. Student Profile

Students can save their academic information.

The profile includes:

* Full Name
* Matric Number
* Institution
* Department
* Academic Level
* Academic Session

The saved information is used when generating payment summary reports.

15. Dark and Light Mode

FeeWise provides a theme toggle that allows users to switch between light and dark display modes.

The selected theme is saved using browser Local Storage.

16. Responsive Design

FeeWise is designed to work on different screen sizes, including:

* Desktop Computers
* Laptop Computers
* Tablets
* Smartphones

The application includes responsive navigation, forms, dashboard cards, tables, and payment reports.

17. Local Storage

FeeWise uses browser Local Storage to save:

* School Fee Categories
* Payment Records
* Student Information
* Theme Preferences

Saved information remains available after refreshing the page, provided the browser’s website data has not been cleared.

How to Run the Application

Method 1: Open Directly in a Browser

1. Download the project folder.
2. Extract the downloaded files if necessary.
3. Open the project folder.
4. Locate index.html.
5. Double-click index.html.

1. Open Visual Studio Code.
2. Select File → Open Folder.
3. Select the School-Payment-Breakdown-Calculator folder.
4. Install the Live Server extension if necessary.
5. Open index.html.
6. Right-click the file.
7. Select “Open with Live Server”.
8. The application will open in your browser.

How to Use FeeWise

Adding School Fees

1. Open the FeeWise application.
2. Click “Fee Calculator”.
3. Select a school fee category.
4. Enter the fee amount.
5. Add an optional description.
6. Click “Add Fee”.
7. The application will calculate the updated total.

Adding a Custom Fee

1. Open “Fee Calculator”.
2. Select “Other”.
3. Enter the custom fee name.
4. Enter the amount.
5. Click “Add Fee”.

Recording Payments

1. Navigate to “Payment Tracker”.
2. Select the fee you want to record a payment for.
3. Enter the payment amount.
4. Select the payment date.
5. Add an optional payment note.
6. Click “Record Payment”.
7. The application will update the outstanding balance.

Viewing Payment Progress

1. Navigate to “Dashboard”.
2. View the total school expenses.
3. Check the amount recorded as paid.
4. Check the outstanding balance.
5. View the payment completion percentage.

Creating an Instalment Plan

1. Navigate to “Instalment Planner”.
2. View the outstanding balance.
3. Select the number of months.
4. Select the first payment date.
5. Click “Calculate Instalments”.
6. View the estimated monthly payment.
7. Review the generated payment schedule.

Saving Student Information

1. Navigate to “Student Profile”.
2. Enter the student’s full name.
3. Enter the matric number.
4. Enter the institution.
5. Enter the department.
6. Select the academic level.
7. Enter the academic session.
8. Click “Save Student Information”.

Viewing the Payment Summary

1. Navigate to “Payment Summary”.
2. View the student information.
3. Check the total school fees.
4. Check the recorded payments.
5. View the outstanding balance.
6. Review the detailed fee breakdown.
7. View the expense distribution chart.

Printing the Payment Summary

1. Navigate to “Payment Summary”.
2. Click “Print Summary”.
3. The browser’s print dialog will appear.
4. Select a printer or choose “Save as PDF”.
5. Complete the printing or saving process.

Switching Between Dark and Light Mode

1. Locate the theme toggle in the sidebar.
2. Click “Dark Mode” to activate the dark theme.
3. Click “Light Mode” to return to the light theme.

Project Limitations

1. The application does not process actual school payments.
2. The application does not connect to a bank or payment gateway.
3. Recorded payments are entered manually by the user.
4. The application cannot verify whether a payment was received by an institution.
5. The payment summary is not an official school receipt.
6. School fee amounts must be entered manually.
7. The application does not retrieve official school fee schedules.
8. Data is stored locally in the browser.
9. Saved information is not automatically synchronized across devices.
10. Clearing browser storage may remove saved information.
11. The application does not support student registration or login.
12. The application does not include an administrator dashboard.
13. The instalment planner provides estimates only.
14. The application does not confirm whether an institution permits instalment payments.
15. The application does not currently provide automatic cloud backups.
